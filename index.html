The Skool Image Cheat Sheet

Every size, safe zone, and design rule you need to make your group's images in one afternoon, with one free tool. Done beats designer-good.

The sizes

Group cover image

1084 × 576 px (16:9)

The face of your community in search, Discovery, and your About page. Big text, few words. This one matters most, so make it first while your energy is fresh.

About page images

1460 × 752 px (16:9 works)

Your roadmap image, comparison image, and any gallery graphics. Keep them all the same size so your page looks intentional.

Classroom course covers

1460 × 1080 px (4:3)

One template, reused for every course. Change the words, keep the layout. Matching covers make three courses look like a curriculum.

SAFE ZONE RULE: Skool crops edges differently on phones and desktops. Keep every word and face in the middle 80 percent of the image, and check the phone preview before you call it finished.

The five plain-design rules

  • Few words, big text. If you can't read it when the image is the size of a postage stamp, neither can she. Seven words or fewer on a cover.
  • One tool only. Canva, free plan, done. Tool-hopping is decision fatigue wearing a disguise.
  • Pick 3 colors and repeat them. The same small palette on every image makes your group look designed, even when each image is simple.
  • Same fonts everywhere. One font for headlines, one for everything else. Matching beats fancy.
  • Plain and clear beats fancy and delayed. Your images have one job: telling her she's in the right place. They do not need to win awards.

The afternoon checklist

  • Open Canva and create a custom size: 1084 × 576 for your cover
  • Pick your 3 colors and 2 fonts, and write them down somewhere
  • Make the cover: group name, one line about who it's for, done
  • Duplicate the design, resize for About page images, adjust the words
  • Make one classroom cover template and duplicate it for each course
  • Check everything in phone preview, then export as PNG
  • Upload, close Canva, and do not reopen it tonight
You are allowed to make plain images. You are allowed to be done in one afternoon. A clear cover made today helps more women than a beautiful one still sitting in drafts next month.
